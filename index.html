projects

RiverGuide

Answers students’ questions from the college’s 2,400 official pages. Rebuilt on the architecture behind America.gov, it uses no vector database: it searches with a small model we trained in-house on the college’s own pages, re-indexed nightly, with BM25 keyword search behind it as the fallback, and the model reads what it finds before it answers.

  • Rampart Edge blocks names and ID numbers on the student’s device, before anything is sent.
  • Every link is checked against the college’s own pages before a student sees it.
  • Nothing students type is logged.
  • The model is one setting: a 27B Qwen on our own RTX 4090, Grok, or Claude.
  • Every prompt, tool, or model change is graded against a 54-question golden set.

Version one handled 9,000+ student chats a week on Microsoft Copilot Studio and cost $47,000 a year less than the vendor bot it replaced. It followed Andrej Karpathy’s LLM wiki idea: a source-cited wiki for explanations, and a SQLite database for dates and dollar amounts, so hard facts came only from a lookup.

irsc.edu

Built from scratch in WordPress and PHP on Pantheon, with custom plugins that connect the college’s other systems. Emergency alerts from Everbridge post straight to a sitewide banner, events flow in from Element451 and RiverLife, degree requirements come from the official catalog, and an AI plugin turns PDFs into accessible web pages. It serves about 250,000 visitors a month, and I cut its hosting bill by 84%.

Workday-to-Banner classifier

Migrated nearly 1 million undocumented or mislabeled document records (946,091) into Banner, saving the college over $600,000 compared with an outside consultant’s quote. Names and IDs are masked before matching, uncertain files go to an approved fallback instead of a guess, and an optional local model only ever sees redacted input.

RiverBoard

Tracks our team’s work with no dependencies: just Python’s standard library, SQLite, and plain JavaScript. Teammates connect Claude to it over MCP, and every project carries an estimated-savings figure.

pet projects

RiverGuide Retriever is Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B fine-tuned to find the college page a student means by RN, BSN, or rad tech. On 942 pages it never trained on, the right page is in the top 5 for 902 of them, against 631 for the BM25 index RiverGuide runs. Abbreviations it had never seen: 67 of 75, against 51. On 523 real student questions it found an answer for 88%, against 59%. It went live in RiverGuide on 24 October, where it searches first and Qwen answers with a third fewer model calls.
