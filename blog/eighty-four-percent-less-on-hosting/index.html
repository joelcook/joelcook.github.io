250k monthly visitors. 84% lower hosting costs.

A hosting bill cut from $50k to $8k, and why I still wanted to go further.

12 Sep, 2026

I cut hosting costs from $50,000 to $8,000 for a website serving 250,000 monthly visitors. An 84% reduction.

I still think $8,000 is too much.

I wanted to take the site entirely on premises. That proposal never got approval. The result I can claim is the reduction we actually made.

That distinction matters when writing about savings. An architecture I would like to run has no operating history. I can’t count its projected savings, uptime, or maintenance burden as a result.

Owning the hardware would still mean owning backups, updates, monitoring, connectivity, and recovery. “We already have a server” is the start of that calculation.

The bill came down by 84%. I’m pleased with that result, and I still want to know how much lower it can go.
